# phone-stand
Phone stand is a simple stand to mount your phone with adjustability.

## What is this?
Phone Stand is basically a simple phone stand to mount your phone as the name says itself. Designed from scratch in Onshape, it allows you to mount your phone all the way from 200 degrees to 0 degree (Actual usefull range: 90-0 degrees). Simple angle change with onboarded knob. Moreover, has a sleek and cool look in orange and white color shades.

## How to get your own Phone Stand?
1. Download the source CAD .step files from the [Hardware](Hardware/) directory
2. Import the file into your CAD editor and make edits as you want (Optional)
3. Go to your fabricater/ manufacturer website, get a quote, and purchase it (For Example, [ROBU](https://robu.in) or [JLCPCB](https://jlcpcb.com))
4. That's it! Wait for it to arrive and go mount your phone!

## Fabrication Specs
1. Printer- 0.2 mm Standard Quality
2. Material- PLA
3. Color- Orange & White
4. Infill- 60%
